ABOUT SJCCC

Preamble: Saint Joseph's Catholic Comprehensive College, Mbengwi is a co-educational boarding College situated in a quiet and peaceful environment free from urban distractions. Our aim is to provide "Christian and quality education".


Ownership: The College is a property of the Archdiocese of Bamenda.


We Admit Students Who Are Ready To:

  • Work hard
  • Learn good behaviour and honesty
  • Abide by the college rules and regulations
DEPARTMENTS & ENTRY

ENTRY REQUIREMENTS

  • First Cycle: A pass in Government Common Entrance Examination.
  • Second Cycle: A pass in FOUR (4) Ordinary Level Certificate subjects with a combination.

THE COLLEGE OFFERS:

  • O/L & A/L Grammar (Arts/Science)
  • Form(1-3) Technical

Technical Departments (Forms 1-3):

  • Building Construction (CE-BC)
  • Fashion Design (FE)
  • Electrical Power System (EPS)
  • Automobile Repair Mechanics (ARM)
  • Home Economics (HEc)

SCHOOL RULES & PARENTS' GUIDE

PROHIBITED ITEMS

The following are strictly prohibited. When seen, they are confiscated, not given back, and the student punished severely:

  • Cell phones, electrical appliances (e.g. iron, digital cameras, radios, walkmans etc).
  • Other dresses and shoes apart from uniforms.
  • No cooked food, garri, contraceptives, cigarettes, and compact discs.
  • All make-up materials such as nail-hardener, necklace, and any other jewelry.
  • Wrong uniforms/shoes will be confiscated. No breaking bounds or scaling fences.

PARENTS AND GUARDIANS

  • All students must appear neat, smart with short hair cut, and no styles on re-opening day.
  • Students not allowed to receive food and chewable from home except on visiting days (Once a term).
  • Visits begin with Holy Mass at 9am and end at 4:30pm. Strictly limited to parents/relatives. Visits outside normal days NOT ALLOWED.
  • Once in school, requests to return home for special celebrations are NOT granted except for funerals of parents, brothers, and sisters in the strict sense of the word.
  • Students whose parents do not live in Bamenda MUST have a responsible GUARDIAN in Mbengwi or Bamenda to take care of them (furnish college with name/number).

HEALTH & FINANCE

HEALTH:
The school has a nurse and an infirmary. Serious cases are taken to the hospital and parents/guardians informed. Students are registered in BEPHA (a mutual health scheme for Catholic Institutions). Come with names, phone numbers, and residential addresses of parents/guardians.
N.B: Students on a special diet shall not be admitted.


POCKET MONEY:
All money should be kept with the bursar. No student should keep money in the dormitory or classroom. Pocket allowance should not be more than 10,000FCFA per term.


MANUAL LABOUR EQUIPMENTS:
All new students are to pay 1,500frs for manual labour equipments (e.g. floor rag, brooms, cutlasses/lances and hoes).
