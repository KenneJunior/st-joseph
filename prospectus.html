ABOUT SJCCC

Preamble: Saint Joseph's Catholic Comprehensive College, Mbengwi is a co-educational boarding College situated in a quiet and peaceful environment free from urban distractions. Our aim is to provide "Christian and quality education". Established on 28th May 1999, the College introduced a Second Cycle with Arts and Commercial sections in the 2009/2010 academic year, followed a year later by the Science section; the Technical section became operational in the 2025/2026 academic year.


Ownership: The College is a property of the Archdiocese of Bamenda. It was formerly entrusted to the Marist Brothers of the Schools and has since returned to the direct care of the Archdiocese.


We Admit Students Who Are Ready To:

  • Work hard
  • Learn good behaviour and honesty
  • Abide by the college rules and regulations

DEPARTMENTS & ENTRY

ENTRY REQUIREMENTS

  • First Cycle: A pass in Government Common Entrance Examination, plus a photocopy of birth certificate, a recent progress card, and (for new students) a transfer certificate from the previous school.
  • Second Cycle: A pass in FOUR (4) Ordinary Level Certificate subjects with a combination, plus a photocopy of birth certificate (new students), a photocopy of the GCE O/L slip, and a handwritten application.

SCHOOL RULES & PARENTS' GUIDE

PROHIBITED ITEMS

The following are strictly prohibited. When seen, they are confiscated, not given back, and the student punished severely:

  • Cell phones, electrical appliances (e.g. iron, digital cameras, radios, walkmans etc).
  • Other dresses and shoes apart from uniforms.
  • No cooked food, garri, contraceptives, cigarettes, and compact discs.
  • All make-up materials such as nail-hardener, necklace, and any other jewelry.
  • Wrong uniforms/shoes will be confiscated. No breaking bounds or scaling fences.

PARENTS AND GUARDIANS

  • All students must appear neat, smart with short hair cut, and no styles on re-opening day.
  • Students not allowed to receive food and chewable from home except on visiting days (Once a term).
  • Visiting dates (communicated to parents in advance) begin with Holy Mass at 9am and end at 4:30pm. Strictly limited to parents/immediate guardians. Unscheduled visits are NOT ALLOWED.
  • Once in school, requests to return home for special celebrations are NOT granted except for funerals of parents, brothers, and sisters in the strict sense of the word.
  • Students whose parents do not live in Bamenda MUST have a responsible GUARDIAN in Mbengwi or Bamenda to take care of them (furnish college with name/number).
  • Parents and guardians must respect the college's rules and regulations, and should avoid contacting students in a way that disturbs their academic activities or study time.

HEALTH, FINANCE & MANUAL LABOUR

HEALTH

The school has a nurse and an infirmary. Serious cases are taken to the hospital and parents/guardians informed. Students are registered in BEPHA (a mutual health scheme for Catholic Institutions). Come with names, phone numbers, and residential addresses of parents/guardians. Students are expected to maintain personal hygiene, manage their own laundry, and take good care of their belongings.
N.B: Students on a special diet shall not be admitted.

POCKET MONEY

All money should be deposited and kept with the bursar. No student should keep money in the dormitory or classroom. Pocket allowance should not be more than 25,000FCFA per term.

MANUAL LABOUR EQUIPMENTS

All new students are to pay 1,500frs for manual labour equipments (e.g. floor rag, brooms, cutlasses/lances and hoes).
